# Chroma Lines — store listing

**Title:** Chroma Lines
**Short (≤80):** Tap glowing lines to slide them free — same colours pass through each other.
**Genre / tags:** Puzzle, Logic, Casual, Relaxing, Brain, One-tap, Arrows

**Description**
A board of glowing, winding light. Tap a line and it slides out along its arrow — if nothing blocks the way. Pick the wrong one and it bumps.
Then colour arrives: lines of the same colour pass straight through each other. A board that looks jammed is suddenly wide open.

- 60 handcrafted-curve levels across six chapters, from first light to 16×16 silhouettes
- Endless runs: lives carry between boards, scores climb, beat your best
- Daily Light: one shared board a day, a new flavour each weekday, keep your streak
- No timers, no text — just light, logic and a soundtrack that plays as you clear
- Colour-assist shapes and patterns for every colour
